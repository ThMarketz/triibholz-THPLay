# Who runs this

*(These details must be completed before the service takes money.)*

This is not a formality. In Switzerland the duty comes from **unfair-competition law** (UWG
Art. 3(1)(s)), not media law, and breaching it is a **criminal offence on complaint** (UWG
Art. 23). For a sole operator, "identity" means the **natural person's own first and family name** —
a brand name such as THPLAY is not enough on its own.

**Operator:** *(the owner's real first and family name. If a company is formed later, its name and

**E-mail:** *(an address a person actually reads)*
**Commercial register number:** *(if registered; state "not registered" if operating as a sole trader)*
**VAT number:** *(if registered for VAT; state "not VAT registered" if below the threshold)*

**Responsible for data protection:** *(name and contact — this is who a parent writes to)*
