# Privacy notice

This tells you what happens to data in THPLAY. It is written under the **revised Swiss Federal Act
on Data Protection**. If your club is in the EU, the **GDPR** applies as well.

## Who is responsible

Your **club** decides what goes in and why — it is the controller. **THPLAY** runs the software on
the club's instructions — we are the processor. If you want your data changed or removed, ask your
club first; they can do it, and we will help them.

## What is held, and where

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Your name, your role, and the passkey that signs you in | On the club's server |
| A player's name, licence number, cap number and team | On the club's server, and on the coach's own device |
| A player's birth year — **only** when they have no licence yet | On the club's server |
| Test results, training logs and development notes | **On the device only** |
| Match video, and cuts taken from it | On the club's server, and on the coach's device |
| Tagged moments from a match, which can name a cap number | On the coach's device |

**Most of a player's data is on a coach's own phone or laptop, not on a server.** That is
deliberate — it keeps it out of a central store — but it means the club must look after those
devices.

We never collect a player's **date of birth**, address, or phone number. We never ask a public
source for a player's date of birth even where one publishes it.

## Video of children

Match video shows identifiable children. It is:

- uploaded by a coach, kept on the club's server and on that coach's device;
- **deleted automatically about twelve months after upload.** On the club's server this happens on
  its own, whether or not anyone is using the app. On a coach's own phone or laptop it happens
  **the next time that coach opens the Film Room** — so a coach who stops using the app keeps what
  is on their device until they open it again. We say so rather than claiming a deletion we cannot
  reach. A coach can mark a specific clip to keep for teaching, and that one stays;
- deletable earlier at any time, on request to the club.

We cannot remove one child from footage of a match — nobody can — and we deliberately keep **no
record of who appears in which video**, because building one would mean matching children's faces.
If you want video of your child removed, the club deletes the match.

## Who can see what

Only people your club has approved, and only for their own club. A coach sees their own teams. A
player sees their own record. Nothing is shared between clubs. Nothing is sold, ever.

## Who else is involved

We use other companies to run the service. They only ever hold what they need to:

| Who | What for | What they see | Now, or later |
|---|---|---|---|
| The server your club's data is on | Running the service | Everything on the server | Now |
| Cloudflare | Carrying the connection to the server | Traffic, not the contents of your account | Now |
| Stripe | Taking payment | The club's payment details. **Never a player's data** | When paid subscriptions start |
| bexio | Invoices | The club's billing details | When paid subscriptions start |

Nobody else. If we add anyone, the club is told **before** it happens.

**The app learns nothing about you.** It notices patterns in a coach's own plays — pass counts,
situations, rough shot positions — with every title, note, name, team and club stripped out, and
keeps them **on that coach's own device**. They are never sent to us.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Ask
your club. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Federal Data Protection and
Information Commissioner (FDPIC), and in the EU to your national authority.

## Children

A player under the age at which they can decide for themselves cannot agree to this on their own.
Their **parent or guardian** agrees, through the club. A player signing in with a code is not
agreeing to anything by doing so.
