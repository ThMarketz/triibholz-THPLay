# Filming and match video — what we are asking

*A template for the club to put in front of players and parents. It is not something anyone ticks
inside the app: consent to being filmed belongs to the person filmed, and it is collected by the
club, not by us.*

## What this is about

The team films matches so the coach can review them with the players. THPLAY stores that video and
lets the coach cut short pieces out of it to show the team.

This is a **separate question from data protection**, and it is worth being clear about why. Swiss
law protects a person's own image as part of their personality (Civil Code Art. 28). Being allowed
to hold someone's data is not the same as being allowed to film them and show it, so we ask
separately.

## What we are asking for

1. That **matches may be filmed**.
2. That the footage may be **kept and reviewed** by the coach and the team.
3. That **short pieces may be cut out** of it and shown to the team, or sent to one player.

## What we do not do

- The video is **not published**, not put on social media, and not shown outside the team.
- It is **not used to advertise** anything.
- It is **not shared with another club**.
- It is **deleted after about twelve months** unless the coach has marked a specific clip as a
  teaching example.

## Who agrees

There is **no age in Swiss law** at which this switches over. What matters is whether the young
person understands what is being asked — the law calls this capacity of judgement. In practice:

- For a **younger child**, the parent or guardian decides.
- For an **older junior who understands the question, their own answer is the one that counts** —
  and a parent cannot overrule it in either direction.
- So for players from about **{AGE_THE_CLUB_CHOOSES}**, we ask the player as well as the parent,
  and we do not film a player who says no.

*(The club chooses that age as a matter of practice. No statute provides one, and a named age is
easier to run a team with than a judgement made player by player.)*

## Changing your mind

**You can withdraw at any time**, by telling the coach. From then on that player is not filmed.

We have to be straight about what withdrawal cannot undo: **footage of a match already played
cannot have one person removed from it.** Nobody can do that, and we deliberately keep no record of
who appears in which video — building one would mean matching children's faces, which would be
worse than the problem it solved. So if you withdraw, the club deletes the matches your child
appears in, or waits for them to expire. Tell us which you want.
