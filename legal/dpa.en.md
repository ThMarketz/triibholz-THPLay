# Data Processing Agreement

Between the **club** (the controller) and **THPLAY** (the processor). It forms part of the Terms of
Service.

## What we process, and why

Only what the club puts in, and only to run the service for that club. We act on the club's
instructions and nothing else. We do not use the club's data for our own purposes, and we do not
sell it.

## Categories

People: coaches, trainers, club administrators, and **players, including children**.
Data: names, roles, licence numbers, cap numbers, team membership, birth year where no licence
exists, training and test records, match video and clips, and tagged moments.

## Security

Access needs a passkey. Everything is served over an encrypted connection. Each club can reach only
its own data, enforced on the server, and anything not permitted answers as if it does not exist.
Sensitive changes need the passkey again.

## Sub-processors

Listed in the privacy notice. The club is told before a new one is added and may object.

## Where data is

On our hosting, in the location named in the privacy notice. Nothing is transferred outside
Switzerland or the EU without telling the club.

## Helping the club

We help with requests from the people whose data it is, with data-protection impact assessments,
and with breach notification. We tell the club **without undue delay** on becoming aware of a
breach.

## Deletion

- Match video and clips: deleted automatically about twelve months after upload.
- A player: deleted outright on the club's instruction — every record, though not footage of a
  match they appeared in, which nobody can edit them out of.
- Everything else: deleted within 30 days of the arrangement ending. This is done on request and
  on our own initiative, not by an automatic process — said plainly because a club's lawyer will
  ask, and "it happens automatically" would not be true.

## Audit

The club may ask how we handle its data and we will answer. We will support a reasonable audit
where a club's own obligations require one.
